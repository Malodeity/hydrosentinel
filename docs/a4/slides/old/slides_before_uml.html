The problem

Nobody sees the whole picture.

Audit data

South Africa's municipal water services are audited in long annual PDF reports.

Residents

No simple way to report a leak or outage and follow it up.

Officials

No single view of which municipalities are at risk and what is being fixed.

Emerging technology

Two kinds of AI. One rates, one writes.

Risk model (XGBoost)

26 of 29

unseen authorities rated correctly (89.7%). It learned from 144 audited ones. A simple rule of thumb got 10 of 29.

Writing assistant (GPT-4o)

  • Replies to residents
  • Drafts a Corrective Action Plan (CAP): the fixes to carry out
  • Answers officials' questions

A person approves before anything changes.

Limitations

The AI usage limit lives in one server's memory, so a restart resets it.Next: keep the count in shared storage.
